PILLSYNC KNOWLEDGE BASE

PillSync is an intelligent medicine reminder and medication tracking
platform designed to help patients manage their medicines and improve
medication adherence.

PillSync supports three user roles: Patient, Caregiver, and Admin.

A Patient can manage personal medicines, view reminders, mark medicines
as taken or missed, track adherence, view prescriptions, and communicate
with caregivers.

A Caregiver can monitor assigned patients, view medication adherence,
check missed doses, monitor reminders, and communicate with patients.

An Admin can manage users, monitor platform activity, manage system
settings, and review reports.

Medication reminders help patients remember when to take their
prescribed medicines.

Each medication can contain information such as medicine name, dosage,
frequency, start date, end date, instructions, and prescription details.

Patients can mark a reminder as Taken when they have successfully taken
their medicine.

Patients can mark a reminder as Missed when they did not take the
medicine.

Patients can select Snooze when they want the medication reminder to
appear again after a short period.

Medication adherence represents how consistently a patient follows the
prescribed medication schedule.

Adherence percentage is calculated based on completed medication doses
compared with scheduled medication doses.

A high adherence percentage indicates that the patient is following the
medication schedule consistently.

Missed medication alerts help patients and caregivers identify doses
that were not taken.

The dashboard displays medication summaries, upcoming reminders, missed
doses, adherence information, and recent activity.

The medication list displays all active and inactive medicines
associated with a patient.

Patients can search medicines by medicine name.

Patients can filter medicines based on status, category, or other
available medication information.

Medication details provide information about dosage, frequency, reminder
schedule, and prescription instructions.

PillSync can support disease categorization to organize medicines based
on medical conditions.

The reminder system displays upcoming medication schedules.

The reminder system can provide notifications for scheduled medicine
doses.

Notifications may be delivered through supported channels such as in-app
notifications, email, push notifications, SMS, or other configured
services.

Caregivers can receive alerts when an assigned patient misses an
important medication dose.

Caregivers can monitor the adherence performance of assigned patients.

The Caregiver Portal provides a view of assigned patients and their
medication status.

Caregivers can review patient medication history and reminder activity.

Patients and caregivers can use the messaging feature to communicate
about medication-related updates.

Users can update their profile information through the Profile section.

Users can view their recent medication activity and adherence history.

The Analytics section provides medication adherence trends and other
health-related activity insights.

PillSync does not replace professional medical advice.

Users should contact a doctor, pharmacist, or healthcare professional
for medical advice regarding medicine dosage, side effects, drug
interactions, or treatment changes.

Users should not change or stop prescribed medication without consulting
a qualified healthcare professional.

If a patient experiences a medical emergency, they should contact local
emergency services immediately.

PillSync uses role-based access control to provide appropriate access
for Patients, Caregivers, and Admins.

Authentication is used to protect user accounts and application
features.

JWT tokens may be used to maintain authenticated user sessions.

The system can provide secure access to role-specific dashboards and
features.

The chatbot can provide information about PillSync features, medication
reminders, adherence tracking, and platform navigation.

The chatbot should not diagnose diseases or prescribe medications.

For specific medicine-related questions, the chatbot should encourage
users to consult a qualified healthcare professional.
